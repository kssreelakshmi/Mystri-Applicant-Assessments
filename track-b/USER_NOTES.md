# Daybreak Repairs | Fictional interview notes

These were written for the assessment. They are claims to examine, not validated market findings. Interview dates fall just after the snapshot; the data itself remains fixed at 7 September, 09:00 IST.

## Owner | 7 September, 10:00

"I think we lose eight hours every week chasing customers. I want an AI assistant that handles it all. We are four technicians and one coordinator. If a tool helps, I could consider INR 1,500 a month, and I could make two engineering weeks available for a first version. I haven't measured what the chasing actually costs."

## Coordinator | 7 September, 10:30

"Most new jobs need a photo or a serial number before a technician can quote. I keep a list and sometimes copy the same reminder twice. A few customers send the photo in another thread, so the list still says pending. I don't log every quick phone call. The times in the export are my active admin minutes, not the time customers took to reply."

## Technician | 7 September, 11:00

"A clear photo and model number help. But an old appliance may have no readable serial label. Some cases wait because a part is unavailable, a landlord must approve access, or the repair isn't worth the price. More reminders won't solve those. A queue telling us what needs a human would be useful."

## Repeat customer | 7 September, 12:00

"Email is fine; I don't want another account or portal. I once got a reminder after I'd already sent the photo. That made me wonder if anyone had read it. Sometimes I just need until the weekend to decide whether to repair or replace."

## Coordinator, follow-up | 8 September, 09:30

"We already use a shared inbox, a spreadsheet and saved email replies. No one has compared that process with a form or approval tool. People also forget to close old requests once a visit is scheduled. Please don't send a reminder when someone has asked us to stop."

## Owner, follow-up | 8 September, 10:00

"I care more about fewer mistakes and less admin than whether it uses AI. I would try a small pilot if we knew what to measure. We haven't agreed on a coordinator hourly cost, expected sales uplift or what fraction of reminders could disappear. Don't treat those as known numbers."
